We use Directus as our brand asset library. Files live in folders; the ones partners may use are in the `Approved` folder. Set up two roles, both with Studio access: `Marketing` uploads files anywhere, edits them, and moves them into `Approved` when they're ready; `Partner` can browse and download only files in `Approved`, and can't upload, edit, or delete anything. A file Marketing moves into `Approved` must become available to partners right away, and a file moved out must stop being available. Partners also download through direct asset links, so this has to hold for `/assets` too.
